#### Scenario: Unfinished source continues after legacy idle
- **GIVEN** selected fragments from the same session, source and selection under the named bounded-continuation V2 policy, without a completed shape supported by that policy
- **WHEN** a later fragment arrives after 1500ms and before the existing 6000ms absolute hard age
- **THEN** the same current unit receives an immutable revision with ordered complete text, and the old raw events, revision and ordered provenance remain unchanged
- **AND** ingest, immediate sentence completion and timer finalization use the same policy; unclosed supported quotes or brackets with internal terminal punctuation do not prematurely finalize it
- **AND** automatic tasks remain restricted to the latest eligible finalized revision; late results retain their exact unit and text revision instead of replacing newer text

#### Scenario: Quoted suffix snapshot growth preserves a balanced prefix
- **GIVEN** an eligible open unit under a named V3 policy, with a fully balanced prefix followed by one unambiguous supported unclosed quote and a nonempty quoted suffix
- **WHEN** incoming text is a strict growth of that entire old suffix within the existing age, byte, revision and reference bounds
- **THEN** the same unit receives an immutable revision equal to the preserved prefix plus incoming text, without duplicating the old quoted suffix
- **AND** ordered old raw spans retain exactly the prefix bytes across references and nonzero source offsets, followed by the actual incoming span; exact UTF8 reconstruction equals the new text
- **AND** old revisions, raw payloads and references remain unchanged, and V1/V2 policies and their proofs remain unchanged

#### Scenario: Policy limits remain explicit
- **GIVEN** generic GDI text without a verified game page identity and a versioned bounded-continuation V2 policy
- **WHEN** the unit reaches the existing absolute hard age, capacity bound, selection change or other existing lifecycle finalization barrier
- **THEN** finalization remains bounded and is committed once, while already finalized text is not reopened and independently completed units remain separate
- **AND** rapid unpunctuated page changes and output beyond the hard age remain documented ambiguity, not a fabricated page reset or a universal full-page guarantee
- **AND** unpunctuated text may wait until the 6000ms hard age for automatic translation, while its source text remains immediately visible

#### Scenario: Quoted growth rejects ambiguous input and invalid provenance
- **GIVEN** quoted-growth V3 input with mismatched or multiple ambiguous quote anchors, an identical or nongrowing suffix, unsupported overlap, missing provenance, or a hard or lifecycle boundary
- **WHEN** the capture policy and single writer evaluate the input
- **THEN** the quoted-growth branch is not used for ambiguous or ineligible text, ordinary legal repetition is preserved, and finalized or incompatible old-policy units are not reopened or mixed
- **AND** invalid source spans, UTF8 cuts, checked arithmetic or reconstructed bytes are rejected with an effective unconfirmed-integrity record instead of a fabricated successful body
- **AND** finalized latest-version automatic task authority remains unchanged; partial redraw and page ambiguity outside the explicit strict-growth rule are documented limitations
