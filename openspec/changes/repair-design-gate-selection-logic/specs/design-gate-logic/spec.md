## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: GLC-001 Actual production impact controls verification obligations
Actual changed production dependencies SHALL determine current design obligations. Pure documentation and unreachable test changes SHALL NOT require product-wide UI capture merely because their metadata says product. A test file imported by production SHALL remain product impact. Unknown production/native/resource paths SHALL retain conservative complete coverage. Code-owned adapters SHALL require exact target, layer, states, command, execute, inspect and independent recompute. Typed readiness results SHALL agree with actual gaps and exit status; missing references or unexecuted production SHALL NOT become passed.

#### Scenario: actual-production-impact-controls-obligations
- **WHEN** actual production, test or document paths and registered execution capabilities are assessed
- **THEN** production reachability and typed facts determine impact and capability, preserving all 794 current obligations and both required real React and packaged-native layers for affected states
- **AND** malformed, stale, fake or incomplete capability and readiness claims are rejected

### Requirement: GLC-002 Current authority selects genuine lifecycle execution
New integration and release lifecycle verification SHALL select real execution from the exact approved current design authority. Historical phase selections and 37 historical screenshot obligations SHALL remain preserved. SR-6 nonvisual, native and four-game runtime obligations SHALL remain mandatory. Preflight, CP01, RBC or historical v4 checks SHALL NOT substitute current-target execution. Missing independent references, mappings or genuine adapters SHALL remain blocking. Closure SHALL revalidate selected authority, states, layers and checks, then read the actual hashed dispatcher interval/nonce, exact adapter identity, source tree and same actual package. It SHALL independently inspect and recompute the raw current receipts; a passed step or receipt-provided package hash SHALL NOT suffice.

#### Scenario: current-authority-selects-real-execution-without-historical-substitution
- **WHEN** a new integration, any registered final cohort or release is verified
- **THEN** both generic and SR-6 final selection use the current authority while preserving all nonvisual lifecycle obligations
- **AND** absent, replayed, tampered, wrong-adapter, mixed-package or preflight-only evidence fails closure

### Requirement: GLC-003 Historical capture preserves strict facts and useful diagnostics
Historical capture SHALL retain all 37 IDs, original reference PNGs, thresholds and failed runs. The five explicitly retired tab/manual-preview-off states SHALL record blocked pending replacement reference without fake PNGs or zero-difference claims. Other states SHALL NOT use retirement as an exemption. Captured Hook events SHALL reach the query declared by the independently reviewed input through visible controls. Preview injection SHALL bind the actual consumer, lease and generation. Scroll selector, top, clientHeight and scrollHeight SHALL all remain strictly compared with individual expected/actual diagnostics.

#### Scenario: historical-capture-facts-remain-strict-and-diagnosable
- **WHEN** historical capture runs against current product components
- **THEN** retired premises are explicit blocked entries, actual captured states keep genuine comparisons, and any missing or mismatched evidence prevents passing
- **AND** query/preview-owner mismatches and all geometry differences remain observable without weakening assertions
