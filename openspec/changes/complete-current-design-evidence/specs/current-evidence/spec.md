## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: GCL-001 validated-independent-reference-catalogue
Current reference approval SHALL be read from actual independently reviewed source-bound catalogue evidence rather than an unconditional absence string or a producer boolean.

#### Scenario: real-catalogue-and-reviewed-state-scope
The gate validates raw sources, recipes, PNGs and independent review, rejects absent or forged entries, and reports exactly the uncovered required states.

### Requirement: GCL-002 executable-current-state-mapping
Current states SHALL receive recipes only where the actual approved design can reach and assert the specific state.

#### Scenario: preserve-original-obligations-and-explicit-gaps
Existing IDs and history remain; source gaps and unimplemented product states cannot be marked passed by generic visibility recipes.

### Requirement: GCL-003 real-layer-execution-and-recomputation
Production adapters SHALL execute registered commands and independently inspect original artifacts for their exact declared state and layer capability.

#### Scenario: actual-current-component-and-package-evidence
Controlled transport cannot claim packaged Native coverage; stale, mixed-source, mixed-package, zero-assertion or incomplete outputs are rejected.

### Requirement: GCL-004 explicit-design-resolution-and-historical-replacement
Visual conflicts SHALL be resolved in independently reviewable candidates before activating a changed authority or replacing a historical reference.

#### Scenario: preview-proposal-and-preserved-old-failure
The candidate separates replay annotations from product facts, adds stale and safe boundary states, and preserves original61 source and37 historical outcomes until explicit review.
